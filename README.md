### Sara Gordic

Design lead and product strategist. I work on interfaces and the systems
underneath them — design languages, component libraries, and the rules that
keep a product coherent while it grows.

Most of what I do now sits where people meet AI: what those products should
feel like to use, and how much of the machinery belongs on screen.

Restraint, mostly. A grid that holds. Type that earns its size. Colour used on
purpose, or not at all.

I build my own tools when the ones that exist don't fit. Some of them will end
up here.
